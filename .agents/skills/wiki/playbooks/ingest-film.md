# Capture a film or viewing notes

Identify the film, year and known creators. A catalog page supports metadata; a review supports that review's claims. Neither proves that the user watched the film or that the agent inspected it.

Use supplied viewing notes or accessible source material. Separate observations, interpretation and review-derived claims. Mark spoiler-bearing discussion clearly; for a spoiler-free request, omit plot revelations. Preserve conflicting interpretations with their attribution.

Use `resourceType: film` and cite external reviews separately where they supply substantive evidence. Follow the [note contract](../references/note-contract.md), [connection discovery](../../wiki-connect-notes/SKILL.md), and [validation](../../wiki-validate/SKILL.md). With metadata alone, capture a pending film note without invented scenes or personal reactions.
