# Maintain the collection

For an audit request, report findings without changing files. For an explicit repair request, apply the authorized mechanical fixes and explain editorial decisions.

Use [validation](../../wiki-validate/SKILL.md) for duplicate IDs, invalid metadata and broken/private references. Use [connection discovery](../../wiki-connect-notes/SKILL.md) to inspect likely duplicate concepts, unsupported links and genuinely useful connections. An orphan is a finding to consider, not a validation failure.

Compare claims and their dates before labeling them stale or contradictory. A newer publication is not automatically a correction. Keep disagreement with source attribution; new external research requires a relevant request. Suggest merges only after reading both notes, preserving unique annotations and updating incoming references.

Do not maintain a second catalog, fabricate links to improve graph density, or add a new playbook for a one-off source. Propose a playbook when recurring input needs materially different evidence or processing.
