# Capture a book or reading notes

Identify the title, author and edition from supplied material or available metadata. A store/catalog link can seed a reading-list source but does not establish knowledge of the book's arguments. Do not infer reading status; include it only when the user provides it.

Use supplied highlights, reading notes or accessible excerpts as the evidence boundary. Preserve page numbers together with the edition when available, and distinguish the author's claims from the user's observations. Do not ask for an entire book when excerpts suffice. Keep raw highlights private.

Read existing title/author matches before creating a source: different retailer links may identify the same edition. Use `resourceType: book`, the [note contract](../references/note-contract.md), [connection discovery](../../wiki-connect-notes/SKILL.md), and [validation](../../wiki-validate/SKILL.md). Mark excerpt-only coverage or pending analysis explicitly.
